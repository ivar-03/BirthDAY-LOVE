# 🎂 Ashwu's Interactive Birthday Surprise Website 🌷

A cinematic, interactive, mobile-first birthday experience built with love for **Ashwu**.

---

## 🌟 The Emotional Journey

1. **Opening Scene: The Mysterious Photo Reveal 🕵️**
   - Starts on a completely pitch-black screen with a subtle breathing pulse.
   - Sudden camera flash and dramatic zoom reveal Ashwu's serious photo.
   - Meme text sequence reveals:
     - *“Wait a second…”*
     - *“Isn’t it your birthday?”*
   - Tap to investigate button triggers the grand transition.

2. **The Transition: The World Turns Pink 🌸**
   - Screen illuminates into a pastel-pink birthday paradise.
   - Fairy lights dangle with gentle warm glow.
   - Falling sakura/rose petals and floating sparkles.
   - Big headline: **“HAPPY BIRTHDAY, ASHWU! 🌷”**
   - Subtitle: *“A very serious production made by a very unserious person.”*

3. **The YES / NO Challenge (Playful Chaos) 😂**
   - Birthday Card: *“Is it your birthday, or have we all gathered here for no reason?”*
   - **Dodging YES Button**:
     - Dodges Ashwu's finger/mouse 4–5 times with hilarious captions (*“Areee, click properly 😂”*, *“Even the button knows it’s your birthday!”*, *“Tapu Sena is investigating…”*, etc.).
     - After 5 dodges, it tames, glows green/pink, and says: *“YES, obviously 💅 (Click me now! 💕)”*.
   - **NO Button**:
     - Opens a hilarious "Access Denied / Identity Crisis" popup confirming she is 100% Ashwu and cannot escape!

4. **The Lights Dim: Birthday Cake & Candles 🕯️**
   - Room softly dims to intimate candlelight atmosphere.
   - A multi-tiered birthday cake rises into view.
   - 3 candles ignite with glowing animated flames.
   - **Interactive Candle Blowout**:
     - Tap each flame to blow them out OR tap "Blow Out the Candles 💨" OR enable the microphone blow detector!
     - When blown out: smoke curls rise, followed by a **MASSIVE CONFETTI CANNON EXPLOSION** and the synthesized "Happy Birthday" music box melody!

5. **Wholesome Surprises & Secret Letter 💌**
   - **Vintage Wax Seal Envelope**: Tap to break seal and unfold an affectionate, heartfelt personal letter.
   - **Ashwu's Official Birthday Privilege Coupons**: 4 interactive scratch/tap cards that flip in 3D to reveal lifetime privileges (Unlimited Coffee Pass, Always Right Privilege, 24/7 Vent & Hug Line, Emergency Snack Coupon).
   - **Polaroid Photo Wall**: Aesthetic polaroid with washi tape and cute handwritten captions.
   - **Star Wish Jar**: Ashwu can type her birthday wish and launch a glowing star into the night sky!

## 📱 How to Run & Share via QR Code

### Option 1: Quick 1-Click Launch (Local Wi-Fi)
1. Double-click `start.bat` (or run `python server.py`).
2. Your browser will open to the website automatically.
3. Open `http://localhost:3000/qr.html` to see the **Printable QR Gift Card**.
4. Scan the QR code using any phone camera connected to the same Wi-Fi!

### Option 2: Deploy to GitHub Pages or Vercel (Free & Instant)
1. Push this folder to a GitHub repository.
2. Enable **GitHub Pages** (Settings > Pages > Branch: `main` > `/root`).
3. Your live link will be `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.
4. Anyone in the world can scan the QR code anywhere, anytime!
